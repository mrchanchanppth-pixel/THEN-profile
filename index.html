Frequently asked questions

Step-by-step guides for using the profile.

MUSIC PLAYER

How do I play the music?

Use the player at the bottom of the Profile page to listen and control playback.

  1. Open the navigation menu and choose Profile.
  2. Press the play button to start the current track.
  3. Use the previous and next controls to change tracks, or adjust the volume with the slider.

SITE NAVIGATION

How do I navigate the profile?

Use the menu drawer to move between the profile, downloads, and help content.

  1. Open the menu using the button in the upper-left corner.
  2. Select Profile, Download, or FAQ.
  3. To close the drawer without navigating, press the close button or click outside it.

BACKGROUND

How do the background videos work?

The page cycles through its background videos automatically while keeping the profile content in view.

  1. Choose Click to Enter to start the experience and allow playback.
  2. Videos change automatically using a smooth crossfade transition.
  3. If playback is unavailable, the profile remains usable while the page attempts another video.

DOWNLOADS

Where can I find downloads?

The Download page is the home for files and resources you choose to share.

  1. Open the navigation menu and select Download.
  2. Browse the available file links on that page.
  3. Choose a link to open or save the resource.

PROFILE

How do I view the profile and social links?

The Profile page brings the profile information and social links together in one place.

  1. Choose Profile from the navigation menu.
  2. Review the profile details and social cards.
  3. Select a social card to open its destination in a new tab.

AUDIO CONTROLS

How do I adjust or mute the music?

The music player includes a volume slider and a dedicated mute control.

  1. Use the slider to set the preferred volume level.
  2. Press the speaker button to mute or restore audio.
  3. Use the play button separately to pause or resume the current track.
