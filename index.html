SITE NAVIGATION

How do I navigate the profile?

  1. Open the menu.
  2. Select a page.
  3. Close the menu when finished.

BACKGROUND

How do the background videos work?

  1. Select Click to Enter.
  2. Wait for the background to play.
  3. The video changes automatically.

Where can I find downloads?

  1. Open the menu and select Download.

  3. Select its link.

How do I view the profile and social links?

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Review the profile and social links.

How do I adjust or mute the music?

  1. Move the volume slider.
  2. Press the speaker button to mute or unmute.
  3. Use play to pause or resume.
